## 🧠 What I Actually Do

I sit at the intersection of **robotics, machine learning, and applied research** — building systems that don't just generate outputs, but *critique, refine, and improve* their own results. Think less "run the model once," more "make the model argue with itself until it's right."

Lately, that's meant:

- 🌀 Architecting a **closed-loop 3D generation pipeline** that fuses vision-language models with generative 3D diffusion — using entropy-based feedback to iteratively sharpen results
- 📜 Applying **Handwritten Text Recognition (HTR)** and **NLP** to transcribe 18th-century court records for an NEH-funded digital humanities project — because apparently AI pipelines and colonial archives have more in common than you'd think
- 🤖 Teaching robots to **infer human intent** from nothing but a nudge, using online Bayesian filtering
- 🗺️ Building **SLAM pipelines** so robots stop getting lost (mostly)

### 🌀 Iterative 3D Refinement via Semantic Entropy
Closed-loop pipeline fusing QWEN2-VL-7B with Microsoft TRELLIS. Custom entropy-based loss pushed text-to-3D alignment from **70–75% → 80–85%**, converging in 3–6 iterations.

### 🤝 Online Bayesian IRL from Human Corrections
A 300-particle Bayesian filter that lets a Franka Panda arm learn *why* you nudged it — updating its understanding of your intent in real time, not after the fact.
